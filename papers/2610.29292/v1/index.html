THE PAPER AT A GLANCE

方法怎样工作

以人工构造的领域专用完全DAG为候选母图（supergraph），用边logit定义采样概率；每步独立采样K个候选拓扑，拒绝包含源节点不可达节点的图。

✓ 原文支持 · 3 处引用
We search for an optimized multi-agent topology as a subgraph of a hand-crafted complete directed acyclic graph specialized to a chosen problem domain (Figure 1 ).
2 Method · b00009 ↗
Each edge e carries a logit \theta_{e} with sampling probability \alpha_{e}=\sigma(\theta_{e}) , initialized to a common \alpha_{0} .
2 Method · b00009 ↗
At each step, we sample K topologies by independently drawing e\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\alpha_{e}) , rejecting graphs with nodes unreachable from the source.
2 Method · b00010 ↗

核验说明：b00009–b00010明确给出领域专用人工完全DAG、边logit及其采样概率、每步K个候选的独立伯努利边采样，以及不可达节点拒绝规则。

执行候选后，LLM归因器比较同题轨迹，根据任务分数、受长度限制的输出、工具调用及最终产物，分配[-1,1]个体信用；减去任务内平均信用，再跨有信用记录的任务取平均，得到节点优势。

✓ 原文支持 · 3 处引用
An LLM attributer assigns each participating agent i in candidate k on task q a credit c_{k,q,i}\in[-1,1] (harmful to helpful) by comparing candidate rollouts on the same task.
2 Method · b00011 ↗
The attributer sees task scores, length-capped agent outputs, tool calls, and the final artifact state (e.g., the coding environment), and runs in parallel across tasks.
2 Method · b00011 ↗
Credits are normalized into per-agent advantages A_{k,i}=|Q_{k,i}|^{-1}\sum_{q\in Q_{k,i}}(c_{k,q,i}-b_{q}) , where Q_{k,i} holds the tasks with a credit for i in k and b_{q} is the mean credit over participating agents and candidates for task q .
2 Method · b00012 ↗

核验说明：b00011明确归因范围、输入及信用区间；b00012的公式支持先减去同任务跨参与智能体和候选的平均信用，再对存在信用记录的任务求平均。

按目标节点优势更新已触发边的logit；指向活跃节点的未触发非源边接受较小更新。逐步剪除低概率边及不可达节点；最终仅从第T步候选选平均分最高者，平分优先成员少、再优先出现早者。

✓ 原文支持 · 3 处引用
Each edge (u,v) fired in candidate k is updated by \theta_{uv}\leftarrow\theta_{uv}+\eta A_{k,v} . Unfired non-source edges into an active agent v receive a smaller secondary update \theta_{uv}\leftarrow\theta_{uv}+\eta\rho A_{k,v} .
2 Method · b00012 ↗
After each step we prune weak edges whose probability has fallen below the fraction \delta of its initial value (the pruning fraction ), E\leftarrow\{e\in E:\alpha_{e}\geq\delta\alpha_{0}\} , and remove nodes that are no longer reachable from the source.
2 Method · b00012 ↗
After T steps we return the best final candidate G^{\star}=\arg\max_{k}\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_{q}s_{k,q} over the step- T candidates, which are sampled under the accumulated priors. Ties are broken in favor of fewer agents and then the earlier candidate.
2 Method · b00013 ↗

核验说明：b00012支持两类边更新和逐步剪枝；b00013明确仅从第T步候选中按平均分选择，并依次以成员更少、出现更早打破平分。

查看原文摘要

An LLM multi-agent system (MAS) is only as good as the agents it chooses to trust: its accuracy and cost hinge on its topology, meaning which agents are included and how they communicate. A single task score, however, does not reveal which agents help or hinder performance. We propose Contrastive Attribution Topology Search (CATS), which searches for compact teams within a fixed supergraph of specialized agents. An LLM attributer compares execution traces across candidate topologies and assigns credit to individual agents. CATS uses these credits to update supergraph edges while progressively pruning weak edges and agents, yielding a small team for deployment. With small open-weight models and matched candidate evaluations, CATS improves mean accuracy on BigCodeBench by 5.3 percentage points over score-only heuristic search while using 54$\%$ fewer deployment tokens. On MBPP, CATS matches the strongest baselines with fewer tokens than the other searches. In a HotpotQA prompt-poisoning stress test, CATS excludes all adversarially prompted agents in all three runs, whereas random search retains some at comparable accuracy. Per-agent credit thus supports both team optimization and the pruning of potentially harmful agents.
